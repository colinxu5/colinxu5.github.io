I am an Assistant Professor of Psychology at the University of Idaho. My research uses statistical modeling to better understand psychological disorders, especially predictive modeling of psychiatric treatment outcomes, aggregation of epidemiological and clinical trial data, and modeling processes of change over the course of treatments for depression.

Recruiting (Fall 2025): I am recruiting 3 fully-funded graduate students and 2 postdoctoral fellows for two Department of Defense funded projects: (A) statistical modeling of PTSD risk, and (B) modeling risk of negative health outcomes in military servicemember families. Positions can be housed in the Dept. of Psychology, Bioinformatics & Computational Biology, or School of Family & Consumer Sciences.
